# Link G4X and G5 notes: ignition, triggers, knock and cams

Part of `g4x-g5-notes.md`, which holds the overview, scope and sources; read it first. This file holds the ignition, triggers, knock and cams differences on G4X and G5.

## Practical use

### Reading ignition, knock and trigger channels
- `vendor-doc` Ignition Angle is the table value plus the global trims only. Per-cylinder and knock trims are applied after it, per cylinder, and are not included, so one knocking cylinder does not show in Ignition Angle; read the per-cylinder knock trim channels instead. This is a version difference from G4+ 5.6.8 (see Disputed).
- `vendor-doc` Overrides: idle ignition control and launch Degrees Absolute write the ignition angle directly, bypassing the table and trims, so table-plus-trim arithmetic does not hold in those samples.
- `vendor-doc` Dwell Time is the dwell actually measured, not the table value. The sign of the ECT, IAT and cylinder ignition trims is the same as on G4+: + advances and − retards.
- `vendor-doc` Knock Level Detected is the level minus the threshold (the global level in standard modes, the normalised level in normalised modes). A value > 0 means knock is flagged and the trims are acting. Ignition knock trims are in degrees, fuel knock trims are in % of effective PW, and the global count is the sum of the per-allocation counts.
- `vendor-doc` With group (non-sequential) fuel, injectors cannot be tied to cylinders, so every injector gets the highest value of that cell across the knock fuel trim tables. Ignition knock trims stay per cylinder in every ignition mode once full sync exists.
- `vendor-doc` Trigger scope traces show the internal driver state, not the voltage at the pin. With a rising spark edge, the ignition trace shows inactive while the coil charges. In inverted or pull-up DI injector mode, the injection trace shows inactive during injection. With drive reallocation, the traces show physical Ign 1 / Inj 1, not the logical channel. Rule out all three before blaming dwell or injection timing on a capture.
- `vendor-doc` Trigger 1 State shows decoder progress: idle, blank time/teeth, first tooth, second tooth, gap test, gap verify, counting and its timeout, extra tooth, post-sync tooth, the double-gap states and the wide/narrow states. With no sync while cranking, a state stuck in gap test/verify means the gap is not recognised (wrong pattern or noise), and a counting timeout means the teeth stopped mid-count. Blank time filters out chatter from a sensor resting on a tooth edge or from a stopped engine rocking.
- `vendor-doc` Cam target convention: by default targets are degrees from the parked (relaxed) position, with inlet normally + and exhaust normally −, as on G4+. Filling in the lobe-centre offsets (help examples: inlet 108 deg ATDC when fully retarded, exhaust 109 deg BTDC when fully advanced) switches the tables to lobe-centre angles (inlet ATDC, exhaust BTDC). Establish which convention a tune uses, and when the offset changes, shift the existing targets by the same amount. The G4+ help contradicts itself on this, so do not apply this reading to G4+ files.
### Ignition outputs and drives
- `vendor-doc` Ignition drives: ≤ 8 on G4X and ≤ 12 on G5. Atom and Monsoon have no 5-, 6- or 8-cylinder direct spark. 10/12-cylinder direct spark and 6-rotor support exist only on the G5 Voodoo Pro. 3-rotor engines have no leading wasted spark. The G4+ notes say knock control and cylinder trims misbehave above 8 cylinders; whether that also holds for G5 10/12-cylinder direct spark is open (see Disputed).
- `vendor-doc` Ignition Drive Reallocation (from G4X 6.24.370 / G5 7.1.370; absent earlier) maps a logical channel to a physical drive. Example: on an EVO I-III plug-in converted to direct spark, drive 4 is the ECU hold power, so cylinder 4 is fired from drive 5. Ignition test selects channels, so it follows the mapping.
- `vendor-doc` Drives are logic level (≈ 20 mA at 5 V), as on G4+. Bare coils can be driven only on plug-ins with internal igniters, on the channels listed in the Plug-in specific notes.
### Triggers and sync
- `vendor-doc` Multi-tooth supports up to 200 teeth (G4+ help: 60).
- `vendor-doc` Sync Tooth is the crank reference. On Multi-tooth/Missing it is counted after the gap (3 means the third tooth past the gap). On Multi-tooth it is counted from the Trigger 2 tooth. Choose a tooth that no cam edge crosses over the whole VVT range, so that all cam edges stay on one side of it.
- `vendor-doc` Cam Pulse 1x: with plain Multi-tooth the cam edge must stay between one pair of crank teeth; with Multi-tooth/Missing it may move across teeth but never into the gap or past the sync tooth.
- `vendor-doc` Cam Pulse Window sync (Multi-tooth/Missing only) accepts a cam tooth only inside a window of set length that ends at the sync tooth. Size the window to cover the wanted tooth over its full VVT travel and to exclude the others; the help examples are 30 deg (fixed cam tooth) and 80 deg (30 deg base + 50 deg inlet swing).
- `vendor-doc` MAP Level sync is for single-cylinder, triple or ITB engines without a cam sensor (Multi-tooth/Missing only). It identifies the intake stroke from the MAP dip while cranking. A dip ≥ MAP Offset below baro means intake; a dip < half of that means the other revolution; anything between is undecided and is retried on the next revolution. MAP must pulse once per cycle, so plumb it to one runner. Wire it to An Volt 1 to see it on the trigger scope, and set the offset between the two dips.
- `vendor-doc` Nissan 360 optical has three modes. Standard decodes all slots and infers the expected widths from the cylinder count (coil-on-plug engines). Distributor applies the same idea to factory distributor engines such as RB20E and RB30ET. Widest Slot is the G4+-style mode and tolerates non-standard discs. Use Widest Slot only if the other two fail: turn injection off; crank with the scope running; count the Trigger 1 slots within the widest Trigger 2 slot (normally a multiple of 4); enter that count; check that both triggers read Yes at steady rpm; then set base timing. G4+ Nissan CAS setups do not carry over directly. The LT1 Opti-Spark page uses the Widest Slot variant.
- `vendor-doc` A high-rpm misfire, or an apparent limiter below the set limit, on a factory 360-slot CAS (SR, RB, 300ZLink and similar) that gets worse with aftermarket springs or cams is usually trigger error from the cam-driven sensor, not a calibration fault. First check the trigger error counts and a scope capture at that rpm. Fixes, best first: a crank trigger with cam sync; an aftermarket cam-mounted disc and sensor; or, keeping the OEM unit, a fresh shaft O-ring, minimal spline play, correct belt tension or tensioner, an unstretched belt, or a replacement CAS.
- `vendor-doc` Some engines need extra inputs or settings. VQ35DE needs both inlet cam sensors (Trigger 2 plus a DI) and VQ35HR/VQ37 needs the bank 2 inlet sensor, otherwise they will not run. The Viper Gen 2 V10 fires unevenly and needs Custom TDC. Some LS2/LS7 engines need the 90 Offset variant (when sync is flaky or timing is far off). EZ30/36 AVCS needs a 360 deg trigger offset (checked on an EZ30R). HR16DE fires 1-3-2-4, and QR25DE uses the same pattern.
- `vendor-doc` Trigger offsets on the engine pages vary widely, and several are unconfirmed: R53 Mini 94 deg; Hayabusa 256; Audi TT 8N 1.8T 279; BMW M50TUB25 −328; 2JZ-GTE ≈ −6; VR6 ≈ 9; 4A-GE 16V ≈ 5. Use them as a sanity check only; a timing light decides.
- `vendor-doc` Reluctor arming tables on the engine pages mostly run from ≈ 0.5 V at 500 rpm up to about 3-4 V. Some are much higher (BMW S52 ≈ 1 V at 500 rpm to 8 V by 3000; S54 1 V to 7 V from 3000; Suzuki J20A 7 V from 3000). A high table is not an error by itself.
### Knock control
- `vendor-doc` The knock action is ignition retard, optionally with added fuel. In every mode, knock is flagged once a cylinder's level exceeds its Knock Threshold table value. In the regular modes the level is filtered on an engine-specific 0-1000 scale, and the threshold must be fully mapped. In the normalised modes the level is divided by a running background, so constant noise sits at ≈ 1 and only sudden rises exceed 1; these modes are less engine-specific. This agrees with the training account of normalised mode.
- `vendor-doc` Correction is applied per event. Regular modes: the gain is in deg (or % fuel) per % over the threshold (20 % over with a fuel gain of 0.5 gives +10 % fuel). Normalised modes: the gain is multiplied by the detected value (level 15 with gain 0.5 gives 7.5 deg or 7.5 %). The Knock Trim Gain table scales the correction by how far over the threshold the level is (1 leaves it unchanged, 0 removes it). Limits cap the total retard and enrichment. Recovery: once the engine has been knock-free for the advance or fuel decay delay, the correction is restored at the set rate; the delays are in seconds or engine cycles.
- `vendor-doc` Normalised-mode transients: when the filtered rpm rate, TPS delta or MAP delta exceeds its level, the normaliser learns faster and the threshold is multiplied by the RPM or Load Delta Threshold Gain (2 × threshold 3 = 6). If both are present, the quicker rate and the bigger gain apply. Delta Hold keeps the raised threshold for N cycles, and a delta level of 0 disables that check.
- `vendor-doc` Normalised false-knock runaway shows as a slow upward ramp on one cylinder, not a spike: above the threshold the normalising maths changes, so a level just over the threshold reinforces itself. The cure is to lower the RPM Delta Level or raise the threshold slightly.
- `vendor-doc` Filters: wide band 10-16 kHz and narrow bands 3.5-16 kHz; off is for testing only. The bore rule and the second harmonic are unchanged from G4+. Mazda 13B/20B/26B rotaries use ≈ 3.5 kHz. The Bosch-sensor 6 kHz and factory-sensor 13 kHz suggestions are the same as on G4+. The G4+ wide band differs (see Disputed).
- `vendor-doc` Window: length + retard limit must be < the angle between the two nearest sparks; there is no G4+ 5.6.5 processing-delay term (see Disputed).
- Knock response (training): the practitioner uses retard only; see Disputed.
### Cam control
- `vendor-doc` Until the startup, rpm and ECT lockouts are satisfied, VVT is uncontrolled and the cams rest on their stops. The startup lockout is timed from > 400 rpm, typically 5 s, to avoid rattle while oil pressure rises. Cam test is also blocked under the rpm and ECT lockouts.
- `vendor-doc` Cold temperature gain compensation raises P by the set % for each deg C below 80 C (1 %/C at 40 C ECT gives P + 40 %). This is the training's cold-oil compensation.
- `vendor-doc` Cam Angle Test → Calibrate fills in the tooth count and offsets automatically (with the engine above the rpm and ECT lockouts). In User Defined mode, separate advance and retard tooth tolerances limit where a tooth may appear before a signal error is raised. Inlet cams can share one PID set and exhausts another, yet each cam is still controlled on its own. This is consistent with the training's remark about quicker setup.
- `vendor-doc` User Defined setup order: banks and cams → sensors and solenoids with PWM frequency → lockouts → all targets 0 (cams parked while checking sensing) → optional lobe-centre offsets → tooth tolerances → Cam Angle Calibration → targets, base duty and PID.
- `vendor-doc` Dual Solenoid valve (BMW VANOS): a second output is driven when the requested duty goes negative, pushing the cam toward base, so the minimum duty clamps must be negative. BMW S62: User Defined, Dual Solenoid, 100 Hz; the test car kept its diode-equipped solenoids by having a Link PDM do high-side PWM on CAN command.
- `vendor-doc` VVT duty voltage compensation is referenced to 14 V; a filter of 4-6 and 250-300 Hz are typical, as on G4+.
### Cylinder trims and rotary
- `vendor-doc` Cylinder fuel and ignition trims can be fixed per-cylinder values or up to 12 3D tables, allocated freely (shared by bank or pair). Ignition trims work in all ignition modes, including wasted spark and distributor, but without full-cycle sync a trim may also act on the companion cylinder (360 deg apart).
- `vendor-doc` On 3S-GTE gen 1-3 (intake manifold over the rocker cover), cylinders 2 and 3 tend to run slightly lean; add fuel trim there and check it against individual-cylinder EGT or lambda.
- `vendor-doc` Setting rotary base timing: first turn injection off; set a −20 trailing split around cranking; lock timing at 0; put the timing light on the front rotor's trailing lead and adjust the offset until it shows the 20 ATDC mark (leading is then at 5 ATDC); turn injection back on; store the offset but not the edited split; recheck at around 2000 rpm. Check that the mark exists: 12A has 0 and 20, early 13B has 5 and 20, and late 13B has only 20.
- `vendor-doc` Rotary wiring: leading wasted spark uses one dual-post coil per leading pair plus one drive per trailing plug (3 drives per 2 rotors), which is impossible on odd rotor counts such as the 20B; leading direct spark uses 2 drives per rotor. Leading coils always take the lowest drives: 2-rotor wasted puts leading on Ign1 and trailing on Ign2/3; 2-rotor direct puts leading on Ign1-2 and trailing on Ign3-4; on 4-rotor wasted, Ign1-2 fire the L1/L3 and L2/L4 pairs. Changing mode means rewiring and a new offset. 13B plug-ins send Ign2 to the rear rotor and Ign3 to the front (unlike G4+): swap 2 and 3 by reallocation, or use a trigger offset near 180 deg.
- `vendor-doc` Sequential staged injection on 3- or 4-rotor engines requires an Xtreme, Fury or Storm (the help names only these G4X models; G5 support is not stated).
- `vendor-doc` Metering oil pump (closed-loop stepper in Rotary Oil Pump mode; RX7Link S6 and S7-8 drive the factory pump): the table is oil delivery from 0-100 %, and the feedback is calibrated across the travel (voltages disputed; see Disputed). Connect the sensor ground to the ECU sensor ground, confirm the direction with Stepper Calibration, and complete this before the first start. A control fault triggers a 2000 rpm limit; No Feedback Selected means no input is assigned. Not available on Atom or Monsoon.

## Disputed / conflicts

- **Knock response.** The practitioner sets knock response to ignition retard only because, in his experience, adding fuel achieves little. That is one practitioner's view; the platform offers both.
- **Knock hardware on entry-level boxes.** Training (Why): per-cylinder knock needs a knock board, which entry-level boxes lack. Help 7.8.2: the Atom pinout and Monsoon notes put knock hardware on V3.0 and later only (on Monsoon V3+ it shares the DI 4 pin), while the Knock Control overview lists the Atom as lacking it outright. The help governs over the training; check the hardware version, and treat any Atom as having no knock input until the unit shows one.
- **Wide-band knock filter range.** G4+ (`semantics-ignition.md`): the wide band is 4-10 kHz or 10-16 kHz, with the lower one suiting flat-response sensors. G4X/G5 help 7.8.2 record: wide band 10-16 kHz, narrow 3.5-16 kHz, and no 4-10 kHz choice. The option was either dropped or is missing from the record; consult the Freq Channel list in 7.8.2. The G4+ statement stays G4+-only.
- **Knock window rule.** G4+ 5.6.5+ (`semantics-ignition.md`): start + length + retard limit + processing delay must fit between the nearest firing events. G4X/G5 7.8.2 record: length + retard limit < gap, with no start or delay term. Use the G4+ sum as the conservative check until the full 7.8.2 topic is read.
- **Ignition Angle content.** G4+ 5.6.8 help: timing after all trims. G4X/G5 7.8.2: excludes cylinder and knock trims. Treat this as a version difference and scope each statement to its version.
- **Above 8 cylinders.** G4+ (`semantics-ignition.md`): knock control and cylinder trims are unreliable beyond 8 cylinders. The G5 Voodoo Pro supports 10/12-cylinder direct spark (7.8.2); the records do not say whether the trim and knock caveat still applies on G5.
- **Idle ignition controller type.** Training (Why; idle ignition channel bullet): PID-style, with min/max clamps. Help 7.8.2 (Idle Ignition Control): PD about an idle ignition target. The help governs.
- **Oil pump feedback voltages.** Closed Loop Stepper / Rotary Oil Pump: ≈ 0.8 and 4 V; Oil Metering Pump Wiring: ≈ 0.9 and 4.4 V. The feedback is calibrated per car, so this has no tuning impact.
