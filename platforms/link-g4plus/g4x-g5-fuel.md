# Link G4X and G5 notes: fuel, injection and lambda

Part of `g4x-g5-notes.md`, which holds the overview, scope and sources; read it first. This file holds the fuel, injection and lambda differences on G4X and G5.

## Practical use

### Reading fuel and lambda channels
- Closed-loop correction reads zero when closed loop is locked out (TPS or MAP rate lockouts, startup delay, coolant lockout). Zero does not mean the engine is on target, so compare measured and target lambda directly in those samples. `vendor-doc`: CL Lambda Status gives the reason (engine cut, launch armed, lambda error, anti-lag or cyclic idle, TPS/MAP delta, TPS limit, startup timer). "Lambda error" means the reading is outside its permitted range, which is likely a sensor problem. On a single bank, statuses 2-4 stay Off.
- `vendor-doc` pulse width: Injection Actual PW is the sum of Effective PW, dead time and the short pulse adder; Effective PW excludes both of the latter. On G4+, Actual adds only dead time, so the names match but the sums differ. Secondary and tertiary injectors have their own pair of channels.
- `vendor-doc` modelled air channels (Load (Abs), Air per Cylinder Estimated g/cyl, Mass Air Flow Estimated g/s) are meaningful only in the Modelled modes (Multi Fuel included), and only with the real engine capacity and a calibrated MAP sensor. They are derived from the VE table, so they move whenever VE is edited. Air per Cylinder Measured is based on the MAF. Fuel per Cylinder (mg) is the estimated air combined with lambda target, stoich and active trims, divided by the staging tables. This is why, in the idle worked example, a low g/cyl estimate points to a low VE cell.
- `vendor-doc` Fuel Calculation status flags fuel pinned at its min or max, injector duty at max, or a secondary plus tertiary share above 100 % (the excess is removed from the secondaries). Not OK means the commanded fuel is not being delivered; rule this out before chasing a lean reading high in the map.
- `vendor-doc` Lambda Target is the sum of the target table and the Open Loop Target Overlay. The per-bank CL Lambda Target Error reads 0 in narrowband mode.
- `vendor-doc` Lambda Avg is the mean of up to 12 widebands, counting only those inside 0.1 to 9 lambda (or a tighter range if the Analog Error High/Low voltages set one). If all are out of range it reads 0, so 0 in a log means no valid sensor, not a lean engine.
- `vendor-doc` start fuel channels: while the engine runs, Prime Fuel shows the prime used at the last start. First Crank Prime shows the pending amount and drops to 0 on the first rotation. Accel Fuel units depend on the mode: ms (Pulse Width) or mg/cyl ×10 (Mass of Fuel).
- `vendor-doc` consumption is an accumulated total (kept through power cycles) built from pulse width, differential fuel pressure and injector data. It includes primes, cylinder and knock trims, CLL and accel fuel, and omits asynchronous pulses and Secondary Extra secondaries. The cc/min instantaneous channel is its rate; the per-distance figure uses Driven speed and reads 0 at rest. Traditional mode needs the optional Fuel Flow table (single-injector cc/min against differential pressure or MGP), switched on and calibrated; Modelled mode uses the injector settings. If the virtual tank drifts away from real fills, suspect the injector data or the fuel pressure input.
### Injection modes and staging
- `vendor-doc` capability by box: up to 8 injector drives on G4X and 16 on G5. DI and direct/port modes exist only on the DI-capable G5 Voodoo Pro, so G4X logs never contain DI fuel channels. Three-stage sequential is limited to higher models, and lower models cap sequential at fewer cylinders. On 12 cylinders, direct spark cannot be combined with Sequential/Multi-Point Staged injection. A missing mode may reflect the hardware rather than the tuner's choice.
- `vendor-doc` injection rate per cycle (four-stroke): 1 gives one event per 720 crank deg, 2 one per 360, and 4 one per 180; two-stroke halves these. Single-point group allows rates 1-12, while the staged group modes allow only 1, 2 and 4. Multipoint group staggers odd against even drives by half an interval.
- `vendor-doc` sequential: drive N fires cylinder N, using the firing order from its table, and needs 720 deg sync. Semi-sequential fires pairs from the first half of the drives under timing control and still needs full sync. Cylinders are paired by firing order, and each pair sits on the drive of the lower-numbered cylinder.
- `vendor-doc` injection timing is in deg BTDC of firing TDC, where 360 is overlap TDC. A typical port target ends just ahead of intake opening, at about 360 BTDC. Group modes reference cylinder 1 TDC with the others spaced evenly. Rotaries use 0-360 deg. On rotaries and staged setups, time on Start of Injection so that fuel cuts behave.
- `vendor-doc` Group/Staged puts primaries on odd drives and secondaries on even drives, with a single timing. With end-of-injection timing the groups start at different times and finish together; with start timing they start together. The primaries are usually closest to the valve and no larger than the secondaries.
- `vendor-doc` Sequential Staged uses 2N drives: primaries on 1..N and secondaries on N+1..2N. Sequential/Multi-Point Staged uses N sequential primaries plus 2 drives running two secondary groups (with the secondaries upstream in the intake). On 8 drives, 5-6 cylinders call for Sequential/Multi-Point Staged and ≥ 8 cylinders for Group Staged.
- `vendor-doc` Three Stage Sequential (from 6.26.11 on G4X and 7.3.11 on G5) uses three sequential drives per cylinder (primary, secondary, tertiary) and needs full sync. The secondary and tertiary tables each take a percentage of total fuel, and the primaries supply the rest (25 % + 30 % leaves the primaries 45 %). If the two add up to > 100 %, the tertiary has priority (40 % / 70 % gives 30 / 70 / 0).
- `vendor-doc` Specified Flow Rate staging mode (Modelled only) splits fuel mass rather than time (40 mg/cyl at 25 % gives 30 mg primary and 10 mg secondary), with flow and rated pressure set per stage. Secondary Extra adds secondary fuel on top of an unchanged primary pulse. It is being phased out and has no tertiary; in Modelled modes it overfuels beyond the calculated need, so avoid it there.
- `vendor-doc` Sec/Ter PW lockout is tested on the stage's calculated pulse including the short pulse adder but without dead time. Below the limit the stage switches off and the primaries take its share. RPM lockouts also disable stages. Status reads Inactive, Active, PW Lockout or RPM Lockout, with 0.15 ms hysteresis once running. If any one cylinder falls under the limit the stage is disabled on all cylinders, so a single cylinder's trim, CLL or knock fuel trim can drop every secondary; look there first when staging cuts out.
- `vendor-doc` Sec Accel Fuel is the number of primary events, after the secondaries switch on, in which the primaries also deliver the secondary portion (such an event delivers primary + 2 × secondary). This bridges the transport delay from upstream secondaries. Fractional values give a partial event (2.5 means the third event adds half). Ter Accel Fuel does the same for tertiaries.
- `vendor-doc` drive reallocation for injectors (from G4X 6.24.370 / G5 7.1.370) is a table mapping fuel-equation channels to physical drives (for example port on 1-4 and DI on 5-8). When it is active, drive numbers in logs or tests are not cylinder channels, so consult the table.
- `vendor-doc` in non-sequential modes (multipoint group and others), per-cylinder trims, CLL included, apply per drive, so "cylinder X" means drive X.
### Injector drives and data
- `vendor-doc` drivers: no current G4X or G5 has internal peak-and-hold. Wire-ins and most plug-ins drive only high-impedance injectors, directly. (The G4+ Thunder, Xtreme and Fury had peak-and-hold.) The named exception is VLLink, which drives its factory 3 ohm injectors peak-and-hold; replacements should be 2-3 ohm. See Disputed.
- `vendor-doc` ballast resistors: an injector of < 2 ohm takes 2.2 ohm, one injector per drive; 2-6 ohm takes 4.7 ohm, with at most two per drive. Current per injector is supply V / (coil + ballast), summed when injectors share a drive: 12 ohm at 13.5 V draws 1.125 A, and 2 ohm + 4.7 ohm at 12 V draws 1.79 A. Never wire injectors in series. High-impedance injectors: at most two per drive, one is better, and pair them only where cylinders outnumber drives (this matches the current-check point under Breaks when in `g4x-g5-notes.md`).
- `vendor-doc` Active Drives (single/multipoint group, group/staged) sets 1-8 firing drives (up to 16 on G5 Voodoo Pro); unused drives become aux outputs.
- `vendor-doc` dead time is 2D on battery voltage or 3D with user axes, with one choice covering primary and secondary together. From G4X 6.26.11 / G5 7.3.11 it may go down to −1 ms, because strong DI injectors may open faster than they close.
- `vendor-doc` Min Effective PW (primaries) is checked after trims against both the effective and the actual pulse. If either is short, the effective pulse is lengthened until the shorter of the two reaches the minimum. Both are checked because a negative dead time can leave actual below effective. The check is per injector when cylinder trims are active, and CLL is applied per cylinder even on one bank.
- `vendor-doc` example data for the Link EV14-type 1000C: 880 cc/min @ 300 kPa; min effective PW 0.25 ms. Dead time at 12 V is about 1.72 ms @ 200 kPa, 1.23 ms @ 300 and 0.90 ms @ 400. At 300 kPa it falls from about 2.22 ms @ 8 V to 1.01 ms @ 14 V. The short pulse adder rises from 0 to about 1.1 ms, dips to about −0.14 ms between about 1.4 and 2.1 ms, and returns to 0 by 3 ms. So pressure shifts dead time as much as voltage does, and negative adder cells are real data, not a dead time mistake.
- `vendor-doc` fault 122, max injector duty: requested duty above the Injector Duty Cycle Fault Value (usually 90-100 %) for 200 ms, on primaries or secondaries. The usual cause is injectors that are too small or fuel pressure that is too low; if it appears only in transients, suspect excess accel enrichment.
### Transient fuel
- `vendor-doc` accel enrichment = sensitivity table × TPS or MAP rate × cold correction. The deadband is the minimum rate that triggers it (for example 1 % TPS per 100 ms). Sensitivity has been a table since G4X 6.16.15. Cold correction is an ECT-indexed multiplier: 1.0 when hot and > 1.0 where the cold engine needs more.
- `vendor-doc` accel decay: once the fresh calculation drops, each injection event removes a fixed % of what remains (50 %: 4 → 2 → 1 → 0.5 ms). This is geometric, whereas the G4+ help decays the original amount linearly. The duration still depends on injection rate (mode, cylinders) and rpm, so G4+ decay settings do not transfer.
- `vendor-doc` Fuel adding type, in Modelled modes from G4X 6.24.370 and G5 7.1.370, is either the legacy extra pulse width or extra mass fed through the fuel equation. Mass accounts for fuel pressure, so choose it when differential pressure varies (returnless or unreferenced systems).
- `vendor-doc` wall wetting compensation (G4X 6.19.37, G5) adds a set % on a continuous basis whenever injected volume exceeds the previous amount, including when coming out of fuel cut. Tune it before accel enrichment, which will then need less.
- `vendor-doc` asynchronous injection adds extra pulse(s) after the main pulse when calculated fuel rises after the cycle's fuel has already been injected, provided the extra exceeds a minimum amount. G4+ has only group-fire wakeup for this.
### Start and warm-up
- `vendor-doc` crank enrichment is an ECT table of 18 cells at 10 C spacing (the G4+ help gives 20), optionally 3D, with a crank hold time. A separate first-crank prime is applied on the first revolution only.
- `vendor-doc` pre-crank prime modes are Key On (at every power-up) and Ign Start Position (the first time the key reaches start; it requires a start-terminal DI). The help takes no side in the pre-crank prime dispute.
- `vendor-doc` power-up states: injection, ignition and DI pump test modes reset to off (G4X 6.30.2 / G5 7.7.2). Idle solenoids stay off and stepper homing happens only with the engine stopped (6.16.27). With a rising spark edge, ignition outputs stay high during a stall.
- `vendor-doc` warm-up enrichment may be negative from G4X 6.16.15.
### Fuel model inputs
- `vendor-doc` the charge temperature table is a % weighting toward ECT (50 with IAT at 20 C and ECT at 40 C gives 30 C). If a cell runs richer than target, increase it (more ECT weight, a hotter estimate, less fuel); if leaner, decrease it (for example 50 → 25). This holds when ECT is above IAT. It runs against a G4+ training rule of thumb; see Disputed.
- `vendor-doc` fuel density: the list now includes E85 at about 0.782 g/cc at 20 C. The per-deg-C coefficients are methanol 0.00149, ethanol 0.00109, E85 0.00107 and gasoline 0.00095. Methanol loses about 4.5 % density over a 30 C rise, so fuel temperature matters most there. Hydrous ethanol needs its own density.
### Closed-loop lambda and long-term trim
- `vendor-doc` banks: up to four from G4X 6.26.11 / G5 7.3.11. Banks 3-4 work in wideband modes only, narrowband never goes above two, and G4+ dual bank works only in stoich mode. Typical uses are two banks for an inline engine with one sensor per downpipe (RB26) and four for per-runner sensors on a four-cylinder. Lockouts are shared and corrections are independent.
- `vendor-doc` typical gain is 2. Trim limits are +15 % / −15 %, or optional 3D positive and negative limit tables (which also make the update-rate table 3D). G4+ training describes one symmetric limit (the G4+ 5.6.8 notes also mention 3D limits; see `semantics-control.md` Disputed), so never assume a G4X tune trims symmetrically.
- `vendor-doc` typical lockouts: a startup timer covering the start enrichments and sensor heating; ECT low about 5 C under running temperature; TPS high about 75 % and low about 5 %; MAP delta about 15 kPa (G4+ figures are under Disputed). Delta lockouts set to 0 are off. Set the rpm lockout under idle unless the target makes idle unstable.
- `vendor-doc` reactivation delay counts samples (G4X 6.21.12), so the real time depends on the rpm-based update rate: 2 samples is 0.2 s at 10 Hz and 2 s at 1 Hz. A slow trim return at idle may be nothing more than this.
- `vendor-doc` long-term trim, Auto mode (since G4X 6.18.8; also on G5): each time the CLL trim crosses a threshold, the nearest LTT cell is nudged up or down, making it a slow integral of CLL kept per cell. LT corr adds to the short-term CLL within its own limits and is retained across power cycles. There are only two LTT tables, so banks 3-4 never learn. The fuel error in a log is STT + LTT.
- `vendor-doc` LTT control: the disable input sets LT correction to 0 and stops learning; Disabled mode freezes the table but keeps it; zeroing the table clears it. Cells that stay non-zero reveal base-table error. To fix it, set the LTT axes equal to the fuel table's, merge the bank 1 values into the fuel table, and put (bank 2 − bank 1) into one cylinder trim table covering the bank 2 injectors.
- `vendor-doc` the lambda target overlay can be gated by a chosen DI from G4X 6.22.8 (before that it was simply on or off). The same release raised the max lambda target to 5.0, the overlay to ±2.0 in 0.001 steps, and stoich to 50.
### Wideband inputs
- `vendor-doc` internal LSU 4.9 controllers: one on the G4X Fury, two on the G5 Voodoo Pro, and one or more on some G5 plug-ins. PCLink allows Lambda 2 = Internal even on single-controller units, which gives a meaningless value with no hardware behind it (the help's example is the G5 Y61 TB48 plug-in). Count the controllers first.
- `vendor-doc` Run When Stalled starts the heater from power-up (Yes) or from engine start. Yes also makes the ECU send at least 500 rpm on CAN, so CAN-Lambda can warm up while the engine is stopped. Leave it off normally and turn it on only for cold start tuning, because condensation hitting a hot sensor tip can crack it. Lambda from first fire needs it, or else a custom stream with a fake engine speed (the supplied file uses 1000 rpm, ID 958) with the built-in CAN-Lambda stream switched off.
- `vendor-doc` CAN-Lambda: at most eight modules on one ECU channel (Link CAN-Lambda mode). Module index 0-7 transmits on 950-957, and 958 is the ECU's broadcast; keep 100, 101 and 959-966 free for programming. Do not power-cycle the ECU during CAN device setup.
- `vendor-doc` exhaust pressure for lambda compensation is a gauge runtime (0 = atmospheric); the sensor-type setting states whether the hardware is absolute or gauge. G4+ CAN-Lambda notes assume absolute, so that setup does not carry over.
- `vendor-doc` sensor temperature is reported only by internal or CAN-Lambda controllers; the LSU 4.9 operates at 780 C. Status Disabled/Diagnostics means control was halted by an error, or the engine is stopped with Run When Stalled off. Watch temperature during warm-up to tell under-heating from overheating.
- `vendor-doc` error codes added over G4+: 16 (heating took too long) on CAN-Lambda, often a supply quality problem, fixed with a 22 uF capacitor across the module feed; 26 (battery below 10.8 V), which can occur while cranking; and 35, which is normal when no sensor is plugged in.
- `vendor-doc` generic CAN widebands can either write directly into the lambda runtime (source Link CAN) or into a CAN analog with a calibration (None = pass-through).
### Fuel pump and pressure
- `vendor-doc` Fuel System Type describes only the port (low-pressure) side. DI high pressure is the DI rail pressure; in DI modes, Differential Fuel Pressure refers to the lift system.
- `vendor-doc` Base Fuel Pressure matters only in Modelled modes set to MAP Referenced or Returnless, where rail pressure is calculated rather than measured (see Disputed).
- `vendor-doc` closed-loop pump pressure (G4X 6.16.15, G5) is a PID on measured pressure with the duty table as feed forward; set the gains to zero while tuning that table. The start-up override pressure replaces the target between 0 and 400 rpm to purge air (some DI pumps need this). From G4X 6.22.8 the loop runs at 125 Hz in closed loop and 50 Hz in open loop (previously 10 Hz), and I and D were rescaled, so older gains are invalid.
- `vendor-doc` speed-stepped pumps (two- or three-speed relays) use 2 % duty and 50 rpm hysteresis. FP Speed is the output duty in open loop, closed loop and three-speed module modes; Fuel Pressure Target is non-zero only in closed loop.
### Multi fuel and ethanol sensor
- `vendor-doc` Multi Fuel Blend mixes the fuel 1 and fuel 2 values linearly for stoich, density, injector flow, charge cooling coefficient, lambda targets and warm-up enrichment (a 75 % blend gives table 1 × 25 % + table 2 × 75 %), so targets can differ by fuel. With Specified Flow Rate staging, enter the secondary and tertiary flows at their own rated pressures. Whether warm-up blends is disputed (see Disputed).
- `vendor-doc` the flex sensor temperature appears as ethanol temperature, and it doubles as the model Fuel Temperature when there is no fuel temperature input.
- `vendor-doc` the ethanol reading is clamped (held) above a primary injector duty threshold (since G4X 6.16.15). Status values are Fault (out of calibration), Startup Lockout and Max DC Clamp. A flat ethanol reading at high duty is the clamp, not a failed sensor.
- `vendor-doc` DI settings: Link ECS uses a rising edge with pull-up on; GM/Siemens a falling edge with pull-up off; Continental a falling edge with pull-up on. The wrong combination gives a missing or false frequency and therefore a wrong ethanol %.
### Direct injection pump control (G5 Voodoo Pro)
- `vendor-doc` modes: DI is sequential with DI rail pressure correction. Direct/Port puts DI primaries on the DI rail and port secondaries on low-side pressure, each with its own timing table; use Specified Flow Rate staging with it. The Voodoo Pro uses external boxes to drive the injectors and spill valve. Set DI Injector drive mode to Inverted (active high, pull-up on) for OE driver boxes (FA20, 2UR-GSE, 2GR-FSE); Standard is active low.
- `vendor-doc` pump types: normally open spill (energised partway through the stroke to start pumping); normally closed (de-energised partway); PWM (full volume with a continuous bleed, on any PWM output); NO Old (start angle only, with the end pinned at lobe centre; for example Alfa JTS, or N73 with Bosch HDP2.5); and jackshaft NO/NC (one trigger tooth per lobe). Synchronous types use spare injector or ignition drives, often through external current-regulated drivers.
- `vendor-doc` the reference is the lobe centreline: the crank angle after cylinder 1 TDC at which the lobe peaks, measured with a dial gauge, with the VVT cams fully home and that VVT channel assigned. On a jackshaft it is the crank degrees from the trigger edge to the next peak, which is larger than the 100 % flow angle in the calibration table. Use an edge at or past the peak, because an edge on the lift flank limits flow.
- `vendor-doc` the pump calibration table approximates the cam profile: the angle before peak for each % of max flow. NO types need only a brief on time and depend on an accurate spill valve dead time table. On PWM pumps, 100 % duty must equal 100 % flow, and an unpowered duty must be defined at 0 rpm.
- `vendor-doc` pump flow % = base effort + effort trim + PI (20 % effort with zero gains gives one fifth of max flow; 20 + 30 gives 50 %). Target error = target − measured rail pressure. Fill in base effort so that P and I contribute little, and set the gains last. When the target table falls, the runtime target drops only at the decay rate, because the rail empties only through injection and a step would wind up I.
- `vendor-doc` before tuning, gather the OEM data: driver type, voltage and current; injector flow; pump type; spill valve dead time and currents; lobe count, profile and centreline; and rail pressure across conditions. Any error leaves the model-based controller poor or unusable. Setup order: low-pressure supply, driver box, injector data, spill valve peak-hold, pump calibration, error margins (turned back on after calibration), then the target table. When tuning, make the fuelling safe first, then step the rail target at 3000-4000 rpm to check tracking. On the dyno, shape the effort so pump output % is roughly flat (which allows lower gains), and do the final fuel tune only with steady rail pressure.
- `vendor-doc` twin pumps run either one rail with one PID or separate rails, each with its own sensor and PID. The Injector Bank Allocation table assigns each primary to DI pressure 1 or 2, and bank 2 has its own PW and duty runtimes. The pumps are assumed identical. The output test runs at 10 Hz, 5 ms; toggle it after changes that need re-initialisation. Faults 118/119 mean pressure was outside the target by the low/high margin for longer than the blanking time. On synchronous pumps, rail pressure is sampled in step with the pump while running.
- `vendor-doc` help examples. FA20: DI about 315 cc/min @ 2000 kPa; port 195 cc/min @ 300 kPa; NO pump; rail sensor 0.48 V = 0 kPa and 4.5 V = 25000 kPa; centreline about 52 deg; 3 lobes; on time 20 deg. 2UR-GSE: DI about 820 cc/min @ 10000 kPa; port 328 cc/min @ 300 kPa; two NO pumps; 0.47 V = 0 kPa and 4.5 V = 27000 kPa; centrelines about 280 and 270 deg; 2 lobes; 40 deg. Audi DAZA jackshaft: 25:40 ratio (0.625 × crank), with the centreline 192 crank deg from an edge aligned with the peak.
- `vendor-doc` Link DI Driver over CAN (≤ 3 drivers at base ID + 0..2) reports fault counts; fault bits for injectors 1A/B, 2A/B and the pump; DCDC %; battery V (byte / 10); and temperature (byte − 50). DCDC % is the boost converter duty, and the DI injectors stop at 100 %, so log it at high rpm with long pulses. Over-temperature and DCDC faults increment the total but not the fault bit words.

## Disputed / conflicts

- **When to calibrate the charge cooling coefficient.** The course and technical session do it before tuning the VE table, because changing it later shifts the whole table. The live-training practitioner checks it after VE and injection timing are done, with closed loop off. Discriminating test: change the coefficient on a finished table and measure how much steady-state VE correction it causes.
- **Traditional or Modelled.** The course practitioner tuned in Traditional and said either works the same once configured; the live-training practitioner defaults to Modelled for builds, suggests Traditional for beginners, and calls the choice preference. All agree flex fuel is easiest in Modelled Multi-Fuel; the practitioner shows a Traditional workaround (a 4D fuel table adding about 40 % at E100 plus a 4D spark table) that gets close.
- **Charge temperature model.** The course practitioner leaves charge temperature estimation off and uses a 3D IAT trim instead; the live-training practitioner uses the IAT/ECT blend table with about 15 % ECT bias at idle as a starting point.
- **Charge temperature adjustment direction.** The G4+ training rule (`semantics-fuel.md` worked example) says a lean trend while IAT climbs means adding coolant weight. The G4X/G5 help 7.8.2 (Charge Temp Correction examples) says a static lean error at a cell means reducing ECT weight. These are different tests (drift with IAT against a fixed error with ECT above IAT), so both may hold, but the shorthand rules point opposite ways; always state the test alongside the rule.
- **Master pulse width in Traditional mode.** A course build on G4X aims for mid-map cells of about 50 to 60 and a maximum of about 80 to 90, and in another step rescaled to put cells near 40 to 50 at boost onset. The G4+ figures (Disputed in `semantics-fuel.md`) run from a 70 to 80 tutorial maximum to the help's 75 to 85 at full power. The G4X/G5 help 7.8.2 (Fuel Tuning Procedure, First Time Startup) gives idle 20-30, mid about 50, full power 75-85 and Master Fuel Trim 0 %, the same as the G4+ help. All agree the goal is resolution without running out of range, and that rescaling the whole table by old/new master value is lossless.
- **Pre-crank prime.** The same practitioner says in one lesson that the key-on prime is usually left off, and in a live session leaves it enabled. Help offers Key On and Ign Start Position without preferring either.
- **Stoich difference to ethanol.** The practitioner calls the petrol-to-ethanol change about 40 %; that is the drop in stoich AFR (9.0 against 14.7). As extra fuel mass for the same air it is about 63 %. Use the mass figure when estimating injector headroom.
- **Ethanol sensor fault default.** The practitioner defaults to about 85 % ethanol on the grounds that rich is the safer failure. This protects an ethanol-fuelled car; on a pump-fuel car it runs very rich, which is still the safer side. No source argues the opposite.
- **Injector test behaviour.** Training says the basic test runs until stopped, at a 2 ms / 10 Hz default (the G4+ basic test figure), with an advanced fixed-count option. Help 7.8.2 (Injector Test) says it fires a set count at a set width and rate, then switches itself off. This may be a version change; on 7.8.2, follow the help.
- **Peak-and-hold on G4X/G5.** Training (Breaks when, in `g4x-g5-notes.md`) says lower-tier drivers are saturated types rated about 2 A. The help (Fuel Injector Wiring and Setup tech note) says every current G4X/G5 lacks built-in peak-and-hold, not just the lower tiers, and gives no 2 A figure. The help is also inconsistent with itself: its VLLink page has that plug-in running 3 ohm injectors peak-and-hold. Working reading: all current G4X/G5 drives are saturated except VLLink, and 2 A remains a training figure.
- **Base Fuel Pressure role.** On G4+ (`semantics-fuel.md`) it is also the fallback when the pressure sensor fails. Help 7.8.2 (Base Fuel Pressure) says it is used only in Modelled modes with MAP Referenced or Returnless and mentions no fallback. This is either a version change or simply not covered; do not assume the fallback on G4X/G5.
- **Multi Fuel blended properties.** Why (training) lists stoich, density and the density temperature coefficient. The help (Multi Fuel Blend runtime) adds injector flow, charge cooling coefficient, lambda targets and warm-up enrichment. The G4+ help blends warm-up only when it is configured to follow Multi Fuel, whereas the 7.8.2 runtime text lists it unconditionally; check the tune's warm-up setting.
- **Lambda Avg when no sensor is valid.** The G4+ help is inconsistent (its 5.6.5 notes say 0, another topic 1.0). The 7.8.2 Lambda Avg runtime says 0. Use 0 for G4X/G5.
- **CLL MAP delta lockout.** Help G4+ 5.6.8 gives about 4 kPa; help G4X/G5 7.8.2 about 15 kPa; G4+ training in `semantics-fuel.md` 10 kPa per 100 ms. These come from different versions and sources, so quote each with its own source.
- **WRXLink (11) staging.** The same pinout page carries two notes: injector 8 is not exposed (group-fired secondaries only), and outputs 5-8 permit sequential staged. Inspect the board.
- **Accelerometer availability.** Training says the onboard accelerometer can give longitudinal g. Help 7.8.2 says it is missing on the G4X Atom, Monsoon and Storm, and that it needs zeroing and orientation. This is a refinement, not a contradiction.
- **Line pressure shifting tables.** The Line Pressure Control table descriptions use the shifting tables in the Shifting state and the normal tables in Waiting. A runtime note on the same page pairs the shifting tables with Waiting. The table descriptions are the consistent reading.
